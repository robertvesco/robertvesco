The Faustian Bargain of AI and Technology

2025-07-11

The Promise of AI

You will not be inconvenienced.

You will not be hungry.

You will not suffer.

You will not want for anything.

You will not die.

That is the future we are being promised by techno-optimists. This loose-knit group of tech CEOs, venture capitalists, academics and their allies share a religious belief that technology can solve all problems. And that AI, specifically, will allow us to have everything we want with few tradeoffs and risks.

However, as you already know, if something is too good to be true either you're being lied to or there is a cost to pay.

In the case of AI, it is both.

In the fairy tale, Legend of Faust, Dr. Faust was unhappy with his life and offers his soul to a demon in exchange for unlimited knowledge and magical powers.1 Only later does he realize that the deal was not worth making. This story gave rise to the phrase "faustian bargain" also known as a deal with the devil because people trade their spiritual being for material goods.23

The Tradeoffs

There are two main outcomes of AI that should matter to humans.

  1. Our physical extinction
  2. Our spiritual extincion

The most techno-optimists acknowledge that (1) is a risk, but one that is worth it.

Sam etc…

So just to be clear they believe it's worth risking the fate of humanity. Presumably they've seen what course humanity is on and they've figured there is no other way to right the ship WITHOUT AI.

I'd love to see that white paper. But that's the problem. It does not exist. The people who are risking our future have not thought things through. What is the purpose of humanity? What are the risks? What would need to do? What resources would it take?
